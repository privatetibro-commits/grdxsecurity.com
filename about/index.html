About GRDX Security

GRDX, pronounced "Guard-X," is the premier brand of iGlobal Security Group. We started in Washington, D.C. in 2018 and now run operations from Sterling, Virginia, near Dulles.

Short answer

GRDX is a security company and the premier brand of iGlobal Security Group (iGlobal Group, LLC), founded in Washington, D.C. in 2018 and headquartered in Sterling, Virginia. It is woman- and minority-owned, licensed in Virginia, D.C., and Maryland, and led by former police, military, and emergency services professionals.

Who we are

GRDX is the security brand of iGlobal Group, LLC. The company was founded in the District in 2018 and moved its headquarters to 46396 Benedict Drive in Sterling as the client base grew across Northern Virginia.

We are woman- and minority-owned. Our team belongs to ASIS, the International Association of Chiefs of Police (IACP), and InfraGard.

Standards every client gets

These standards apply on every contract, large or small.

  • Every officer licensed and verified before being scheduled
  • Class A uniform on standing posts
  • A supervisor available 24/7
  • Any officer replaced on request
  • GPS-verified patrol stops
  • A reply to every request within one business day
  • A site walk, then a written proposal

Clients we work for

Our clients include technology companies and corporate campuses, HOAs and gated communities, luxury apartments and condos, retail and commercial properties, and high-net-worth homes and estates.

Our service area covers Northwest D.C., Montgomery County, Arlington, Alexandria, Fairfax, Loudoun, and Prince William, with secondary coverage in parts of Maryland.

Licenses and registrations

GRDX is licensed in Virginia through DCJS, in D.C. through the Security Officers Licensing Branch, and in Maryland through the Maryland State Police. The GRDX trademark application is Serial No. 50136847.
