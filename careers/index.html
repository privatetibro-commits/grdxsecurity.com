Short answer

GRDX careers include security guard, security officer, investigator, and police personnel roles in Virginia, Washington, D.C., and Maryland. Applicants pass GRDX testing and a background check. Current iGlobal officers are eligible for GRDX units and get a leg up over outside applicants, but must meet the same standards.

Roles we hire for

We hire for four kinds of positions, and openings vary by location and season.

Security guards

Fixed posts at buildings, communities, and retail properties.

Security officers

Patrol, access control, and event work across our service area.

Investigators

Case work supporting clients and our investigations practice.

Police personnel

Special police and Police Services roles under experienced leadership.

What we expect from every hire

The standard our clients see starts with who we hire. Officers wear Class A uniform, show up on time, write clear reports, and treat residents and visitors with respect.

Every hire is licensed and verified before being scheduled on a post.

  • Valid license for the jurisdiction, or eligibility to obtain one
  • Pass GRDX testing
  • Pass a background check
  • Professional appearance and conduct

For current iGlobal officers

If you already work for iGlobal Security Group, you are eligible for GRDX units and get a leg up over outside applicants. That advantage is real, but it does not replace the bar.

iGlobal officers still pass GRDX standards, testing, and the background check before moving into a GRDX unit.

Who you will work for

Our leaders include an Army military police veteran, a former D.C. police officer, an FBI-certified hostage negotiator, and a retired sheriff's office Inspector General. You will learn from people who have done this work for decades.

A supervisor is on duty around the clock, so no officer is left alone on a post without backup on the phone.

How to apply

Reach us through the contact page or call (833) 350-4739 and ask about open positions. Tell us your licenses, your experience, and which state or district you want to work in.

Questions

Is GRDX hiring security guards?+

Yes. We hire guards, officers, investigators, and police personnel in Virginia, D.C., and Maryland. Openings vary by location.

I work for iGlobal. Can I move to GRDX?+

iGlobal officers are eligible for GRDX units and get a leg up over outside applicants. You still need to pass GRDX standards, testing, and the background check.

Do I need a security license to apply?+

Every officer is licensed for the jurisdiction before being scheduled. Tell us what you hold and we will explain what is needed.

Is there a background check?+

Yes. Every applicant passes a background check and GRDX testing.
