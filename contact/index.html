Short answer

To contact GRDX, call (833) 350-4739, which is answered 24/7, or send the contact form with your name, organization, phone, email, service, city and state, and a short message. Expect an answer within one business day. For an emergency, call 911, not the form.

If this is an emergency

If someone is in danger or a crime is in progress, call 911 now. Then call our 24/7 line at (833) 350-4739 if you are a client.

Please do not use the form for anything urgent. Forms are read during business hours.

The form asks for a few details so the right person replies with useful answers.

What happens after you write

Someone from our team replies within one business day. If the property is in our service area, we set a time to walk the site with you.

After the walk you receive a written proposal with posts, hours, and the officer profile we recommend.

  • Reply within one business day
  • Site walk at your property
  • Written proposal

Helpful details to include

The more we know before we call, the faster the site walk goes. Include the property type, rough size, the hours you want covered, and anything that has already happened, such as break-ins, trespassing, or a specific person causing trouble.

If you manage several properties, list them all. We can walk them on the same day when they are close together.

Our office

GRDX is headquartered at 46396 Benedict Drive, Suite 200, Sterling, VA 20164, off Route 28 near Dulles. Please call before visiting so someone is there to meet you.

You can also find us at grdx.co and @grdxhq on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, X, and LinkedIn, plus our Nextdoor page, GRDX.
