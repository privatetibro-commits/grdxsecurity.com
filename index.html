A day on post

What you're paying for, hour by hour

A typical day shift at a Reston office building. Every step leaves a record your property manager can read.

06:45

Briefing and inspection

A supervisor checks each officer's license, uniform and gear before they leave for the site. Class A, shirt tucked, boots done. Anyone who doesn't pass doesn't go.

  • License verified for the jurisdiction
  • Post orders reviewed for any changes
07:00

On post

The officer relieves the night shift, reads the pass-down log and walks the building with them. Your named contact knows who's on.

  • Shift handover recorded
  • Radio and phone check with dispatch
13:30

Rounds, logged

Rounds run on a randomized schedule so nobody can time them. Each checkpoint is scanned and GPS-stamped. Anything odd gets photographed on the spot.

  • Doors, fences, garage levels, roof access
  • A supervisor drops in unannounced
18:55

Report in your inbox

Before the evening officer has settled in, the day's report is waiting: patrol times, incidents, photos, and anything that needs a work order.

  • Incidents with photos and times
  • Maintenance issues flagged
Our standards

Six things we don't compromise on

These came out of watching other security companies lose contracts. Each one is written into our post orders.

Licensed where they work

Every officer holds the license for the jurisdiction they're posted in, and we verify it before they go on the schedule.

Awake, sharp, off the phone

Class A uniform, inspected. Supervisors make surprise visits, and the small things get corrected first.

A real person at 2 a.m.

You get one named contact. If you need an executive, they'll pick up too.

Swap an officer, no questions

If someone isn't a good fit for your site, tell us. They're replaced. You don't need to prove anything went wrong.

Proof of every round

FAQ

Questions people ask on the first call

What areas does GRDX cover?+

Northern Virginia, Washington D.C. and Maryland. In Virginia that's Loudoun, Fairfax, Arlington, Alexandria and Prince William. In Maryland, Montgomery, Prince George's and Baltimore.

Do you provide armed security for churches?+

Yes. We post armed and unarmed officers for services, conferences and events, and we can train your volunteer safety team.

What's the difference between a security guard and a Special Police Officer?+

Special Police Officers are commissioned and can arrest on the property they protect. Guards observe and report. We'll tell you which one your site needs.

How fast can you start?+

We reply within one business day, and urgent coverage can often be staffed sooner. For an emergency, call (833) 350-4739.

Is GRDX the same company as iGlobal Security Group?+

Yes. GRDX is a brand of iGlobal Security Group, with the same licenses and the same command staff.
