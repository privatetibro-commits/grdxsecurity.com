Every patrol stop is GPS-verified, so a board member in Brambleton or a property manager in Ashburn can see when the officer arrived and left. Officers wear Class A uniform on standing posts, and the supervisor on duty answers the 24/7 line directly.

Events and seasonal work in Loudoun
