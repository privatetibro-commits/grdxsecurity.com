Questions

Before you call

Who does the officer report to?

The person running the job, not only the office. We agree gate codes, lock-up times and who to call with your superintendent.

Will officers chase intruders?

No. Our officers observe, document and call police. They do not chase anyone across a site full of open trenches and rebar.

Can coverage change as the job moves?

Yes. We set coverage for the phase you are in. It can grow when units go up and stop when the last one closes.

How fast will I hear back?

The phone line is answered 24/7. Send the site address and we reply within one business day, visit the site and send a written proposal.
