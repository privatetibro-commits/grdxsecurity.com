Questions

Before you call

How does a patrol visit work?

The officer arrives, walks the checkpoints we agreed (gates, doors, dumpsters, lots), the GPS records the visit, and anything off is photographed and written up.

What happens if something is wrong?

Serious problems go to the supervisor and to you right away, not in the morning.

Can patrol be combined with a standing officer?

Yes. A standing officer holds the desk or gate while a patrol car covers outer lots and the back fence. Both report to the same supervisor.

How fast will I hear back?

The phone line is answered 24/7. Form requests get a reply within one business day.
